    Introduce circuit breaker for downstream calls

    The payment gateway occasionally becomes unresponsive for several
    minutes at a time. When that happens every request thread blocks on
    the socket read, exhausting the pool and cascading the outage to the
    rest of the service. This change wraps the gateway client in a
    circuit breaker that trips after five consecutive failures.
